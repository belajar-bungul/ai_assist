# Ara AI Copilot Assistant - Modern Enterprise AI for Odoo 18

Transform your enterprise operations with Ara AI Copilot Assistant: an autonomous, multi-provider AI assistant embedded directly into Odoo 18.

## Highlights
- **Search & Read Anything**: Natural language business data queries with instant answers.
- **Safe Approval Step**: Built-in visual approval cards before creating or updating records, with database savepoint rollback.
- **Full Financial Reporting**: Instant Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Aged Receivables, and Aged Payables.
- **Run Actions & Send Emails**: Execute server actions, confirm orders, and send customer emails via chat.
- **Multimodal AI**: Upload images, PDFs, and documents with OCR and semantic indexing (RAG).
- **Smart Navigation & Context Awareness**: Automatically identifies the active model, record ID, and view.
- **5+ AI Providers**: Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, NVIDIA NIM, and OpenRouter.
- **Porcelain Pastel Aesthetic**: Ergonomic luxury design matching Ara Dashboard.
